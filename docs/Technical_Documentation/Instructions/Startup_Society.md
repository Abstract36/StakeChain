Creating a startup society and progressing towards becoming a network state involves several key steps. Here's a step-by-step guide based on the provided information:

**Step 1: Found a Startup Society**
- Establish an online community with a clear vision and aspirations for something greater. This community serves as the foundation for your future network state.
- Anyone can found a startup society; legitimacy comes from people choosing to follow your vision.

**Step 2: Organize for Collective Action**
- Transform your online community into a network union, which coordinates members for mutual benefit and a common cause.
- Unlike traditional unions, a network union isn't solely opposed to a specific corporation. It can engage in various collective actions, such as crowdfunding, job placement, bulk purchasing, and negotiation with corporations and states.

**Step 3: Build Trust Offline and Cryptoeconomy Online**
- Host in-person meetups in the physical world, starting with smaller gatherings and gradually increasing scale and duration.
- Simultaneously, develop an internal economy using cryptocurrency to facilitate transactions and cooperation within the community.

**Step 4: Crowdfund Physical Nodes**
- Once trust has been established and sufficient funds have been accumulated, initiate crowdfunding campaigns to acquire physical assets such as apartments, houses, and even entire towns.
- These physical assets will serve as co-living communities for your digital citizens.

**Step 5: Digitally Connect Physical Communities**
- Connect the acquired physical nodes into a network archipelago, a network of digitally connected physical territories distributed globally.
- Grant physical access through web3 cryptopassports and use mixed reality technology to bridge the online and offline worlds seamlessly.

**Step 6: Conduct an On-Chain Census**
- As your society scales, perform a cryptographically auditable census to demonstrate the growth of your population, income, and real-estate footprint.
- This census serves as evidence of your society's traction and legitimacy.

**Step 7: Gain Diplomatic Recognition**
- With a growing population and economy comparable to legacy states, work towards negotiating diplomatic recognition from at least one existing government.
- Gradually increase sovereignty, ultimately becoming a fully recognized network state, potentially even gaining recognition from international bodies like the United Nations.

**Key Notes:**
- The core concept is to transition from an online community to physical communities while maintaining ideological alignment.
- Network states are geographically decentralized, with citizens scattered worldwide but united by shared values.
- As your startup society's population and income grow, seek recognition from existing sovereign entities and international organizations.

Remember that this process may take time, persistence, and adaptability. Building trust, a strong community, and a functional cryptoeconomy are essential steps to success.
