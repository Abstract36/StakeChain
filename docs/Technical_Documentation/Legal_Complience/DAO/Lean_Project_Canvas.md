Creating a Lean Society Logical Canvas for a Decentralized Application (DApp) focused on a Multichain 2nd Layer Staking/Swap Engine with a goal to address social problems and emphasize key values such as transparency and trust:

The Lean Society Logical Canvas provides a high-level overview of the decentralized application's objectives, target audience, distribution channels, metrics for success, and potential income streams to sustain and grow the project while addressing societal problems and upholding key values.

### Problem Statement:

**Social Problems:**
1. **Trustless Society:** Lack of trust in centralized systems and institutions.
2. **High Inflation:** Rapid devaluation of fiat currencies.
3. **Transactional Values:** Difficulty in transacting value across different chains.
4. **Token Conversions:** Inefficient and costly token conversions between chains.
5. **Transactional Data Ownership:** Lack of control and ownership over personal transactional data.

### Key Values:

**Key Values:**
1. **Transparency:** Ensuring all transactions and operations are publicly verifiable.
2. **Trust:** Building trust through the use of smart contracts and decentralized technology.

### Solution:

**Decentralized Application (DApp):**
- A Multichain 2nd Layer Staking/Swap Engine that provides a trustless, transparent, and efficient platform for users to stake and swap various cryptocurrencies across multiple blockchains.

### Early Participants:

1. **Stakers:** Users who provide liquidity and stake their tokens on the platform to earn rewards.
2. **Traders:** Users who utilize the platform for token swaps.
3. **Developers:** Those who contribute to the development of the DApp.
4. **Governance Token Holders:** Individuals holding governance tokens for decision-making.
5. **Validators:** Responsible for validating transactions and maintaining network security.

### Product Distribution Channels:

1. **Blockchain Communities:** Engage with cryptocurrency communities on different chains.
2. **Developer Networks:** Attract developers through blockchain development communities.

### Key Metrics for Progress:

1. **Total Value Locked (TVL):** Measure the amount of assets staked on the platform.
2. **Daily Transaction Volume:** Track the volume of token swaps and staking activities.
3. **User Growth:** Monitor the number of new users joining the platform.
4. **Token Price:** Evaluate the performance of the governance token.
5. **Security Metrics:** Monitor network validator performance.

### Existing Marketing Alternatives:
1. **Kyberswap:** Partner with Kyberswap for cross-promotion and liquidity sharing.
2. **Rhino:** Collaborate with Rhino to offer insurance solutions for users.
3. **Curve:** Explore partnerships with Curve for stablecoin swaps.
4. **Balancer:** Collaborate with Balancer for automated portfolio management solutions.

### Society Treasury Income Streams:

1. **Transaction Fees:** A percentage of transaction fees generated on the platform.
2. **Staking Rewards:** A portion of the rewards generated by staked assets.
3. **Governance Proposal Fees:** Fees collected for submitting and voting on governance proposals.
4. **Partnerships and Collaborations:** Income from partnerships with other blockchain projects.
