Certainly, you can add "DAO Token Holders" as an additional category to the StakeChain Society Membership Criteria:

### Membership Criteria:

1. **Early Adopters:**
   - Individuals who have actively used the StakeChain platform during its initial stages.
   - Early adopters who have provided feedback, suggestions, or contributed to the platform's growth.
   - Those who have demonstrated a commitment to supporting and promoting StakeChain.

2. **Developers That Help Us Grow:**
   - Developers who have actively contributed to the development, improvement, or security of the StakeChain platform.
   - Individuals or teams who have created valuable integrations, tools, or applications that enhance the StakeChain ecosystem.
   - Active participants in the StakeChain developer community, including those who provide code contributions, bug reports, or technical assistance.

3. **Token Holders:**
   - Individuals who hold a minimum amount of StakeChain's governance or utility tokens.
   - Token holders who actively participate in the governance and decision-making processes of StakeChain.
   - Those who have a vested interest in the success and sustainability of StakeChain.

4. **DAO (Decentralized Autonomous Organization) Token Holders:**
   - Individuals or entities holding tokens that represent voting power and influence within the StakeChain DAO.
   - DAO token holders who actively participate in DAO governance proposals, voting, and decision-making.
   - Those who contribute to shaping the direction and policies of StakeChain through the DAO.

### Membership Benefits:

- **Voting Rights:** Members may have the opportunity to participate in governance decisions, such as protocol upgrades, parameter adjustments, or strategic directions.

- **Access to Early Features:** Exclusive access to new features, products, or services launched by StakeChain.

- **Community Engagement:** Members will be invited to participate in community discussions, events, and initiatives.

- **Development Opportunities:** Developers may receive support and resources to further develop and integrate with StakeChain.

- **Recognition:** Acknowledgment and recognition within the StakeChain community for contributions and support.

- **Rewards and Incentives:** Members may be eligible for rewards, incentives, or discounts related to StakeChain services.

Including DAO token holders expands the inclusivity of the StakeChain Society, recognizing the importance of decentralized governance in the project's decision-making process and strengthening the connection between the project and its community.
