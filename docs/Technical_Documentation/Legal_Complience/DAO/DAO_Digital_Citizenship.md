# StakeChain Digital Citizenship Model Code

## Introduction

The StakeChain Digital Citizenship Model is designed to engage individuals, instill a sense of belonging, and promote active participation in our decentralized community. It empowers members to shape the direction of StakeChain and choose their preferred regime for common life governance within the Web3 ecosystem.

## Core Principles

### 1. Inclusivity

We welcome all individuals who share our values and are committed to the principles of decentralization, trust, collaboration, innovation, and transparency. StakeChain is an inclusive and diverse community.

### 2. Opt-In Governance

Citizenship in StakeChain is voluntary and opt-in. Individuals can choose to participate in governance decisions, contribute to the ecosystem, and influence the direction of the community.

### 3. Self-Determination

We believe in self-determination, where members have the right to collectively shape the rules, policies, and governance structures that govern common life within StakeChain.

## Digital Citizenship Tiers

### 1. StakeChain Residents

Anyone interested in joining StakeChain can become a StakeChain Resident by following a simple onboarding process. Residents have access to basic features of the ecosystem but do not have governance rights.

### 2. StakeChain Members

To become a StakeChain Member, residents must actively participate in the community, contribute value, and uphold the code of conduct. Members gain voting rights and can propose and vote on governance decisions.

### 3. StakeChain Citizens

StakeChain Citizens are active members who have demonstrated a long-term commitment to the community. They may hold additional privileges and responsibilities, such as serving as validators or participating in specialized governance committees.

## Becoming a Digital Citizen

1. **Onboarding:** Individuals start as StakeChain Residents by participating in our DEFI protocol. 

2. **Active Participation:** Residents can become StakeChain Members by actively participating in community discussions, contributing to development, or providing liquidity to the ecosystem.

3. **Long-Term Commitment:** StakeChain Members can be nominated or self-nominate for StakeChain Citizenship based on their long-term commitment, contributions, and reputation within the community.

4. **Governance Engagement:** StakeChain Citizens actively engage in governance discussions, propose and vote on changes, and help shape the future of StakeChain.

## Governance Regimes

StakeChain recognizes that members may have different preferences for governance structures. Therefore, members have the freedom to choose between various governance regimes offered within the StakeChain ecosystem.

## Cultural Stewardship

Every StakeChain Digital Citizen is a cultural steward, responsible for upholding our values, principles, and the integrity of the community. Stewards actively engage in education, mentorship, and support for fellow citizens.

This Digital Citizenship Model empowers individuals to become active members and citizens of StakeChain, offering them a say in governance, participation in a regime of their choice, and a role in shaping the future of our decentralized community.
